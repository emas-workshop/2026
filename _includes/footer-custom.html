About the logo. The logo was conceived and designed by Matteo Baldoni, then refined to its final form with the help of generative AI.

According to the creator: “The logo represents Aphrodite’s shell: Paphos, the host city of EMAS 2026, is known as the birthplace of Aphrodite, born from a shell. Aphrodite’s shell emerges from a network of agents interacting with each other. Towards the centre of the shell the agents are no longer distinguishable, because they are so strongly connected they behave as a single collective and more capable entity. The colours are teal for the Mediterranean Sea, deep blue for the depths of the sea and of research, and golden yellow for the sandy tones to recall the color of Cyprus in its flag.”
